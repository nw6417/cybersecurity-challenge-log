# Cybersecurity Risk Register

# Purpose

This risk register identifies potential risks to my cybersecurity challenge log, rates their likelihood and impact, and explains why each risk matters.

# Rating Scale

1: Low

2: Low–Moderate

3: Moderate

4: High

5: Very High

# Risk 1: Sensitive Information Exposure

Likelihood: 3/5

Impact: 4/5

Risk Score: 12/25

Justification: I could accidentally include private or sensitive information in a public challenge write-up.

Mitigation: Review write-ups before committing them and remove sensitive information.

# Risk 2: Unauthorized GitHub Account Access

Likelihood: 2/5

Impact: 5/5

Risk Score: 10/25

Justification: Someone could gain access to my account and modify or delete my project.

Mitigation: Use a strong, unique password and enable two-factor authentication.

# Risk 3: Accidental File Deletion

Likelihood: 3/5

Impact: 3/5

Risk Score: 9/25

Justification: I could accidentally delete important project files or challenge write-ups.

Mitigation: Use Git commits to track changes and restore earlier versions when needed.

# Risk 4: Incorrect Cybersecurity Information

Likelihood: 3/5

Impact: 3/5

Risk Score: 9/25

Justification: I could misunderstand a challenge or rely on an incorrect AI response.

Mitigation: Verify answers and test my reasoning before adding information to a write-up.

# Risk 5: Exposed Passwords or Access Tokens

Likelihood: 2/5

Impact: 5/5

Risk Score: 10/25

Justification: Accidentally committing credentials could allow someone to access an account or service.

Mitigation: Never include passwords, API keys, or access tokens in project files.

# Priority

The highest-priority risks are sensitive information exposure, unauthorized account access, and exposed credentials because they could have serious consequences. I will reduce these risks by reviewing files, securing my account, and keeping credentials out of the repository.
