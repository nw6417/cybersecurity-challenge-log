Learning Center

The CIA Triad

Confidentiality: Keep information private.

Integrity: Keep information accurate and protected from unauthorized changes.

Availability: Keep systems and information accessible when needed.

Threat Modeling

Identify what you need to protect, who might threaten it, and how you can reduce the risk.

Passwords and Authentication

Store passwords using secure salted password hashes, not plaintext passwords.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) adds another layer of protection to a login.

Hashing, Encryption, and Encoding

Hashing: Creates a one-way fingerprint of data.

Encryption: Protects data using a key so it can be decrypted by someone with the right key.

Encoding: Changes data into another format. Base64 is encoding, not encryption.

Digital Signatures

Digital signatures help verify who signed data and whether it has changed.
